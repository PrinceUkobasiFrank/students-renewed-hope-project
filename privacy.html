Privacy

What we collect, and why

Short version: only what's needed to organize the network, nothing more.

What we collect

  • Name and email — to identify your profile and confirm registration.
  • State and institution — to connect you to the right community and give coordinators an accurate count.
  • Level — used only for aggregate statistics.
  • Phone number — optional. We only ask for it if it's actually useful, not by default.
  • Profile photo — optional, used only if you generate a supporter card.

What we don't collect

  • National Identification Number (NIN) — never. There's no scenario in this platform that requires it.
  • Voter registration data — this platform is not part of, and does not replace, the official voter registration process.
  • Payment information — the platform has no payment features.

Who can see your data

Your profile is visible to your state coordinator and national administrators, for the purpose of running the network. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or made public. Your supporter card, once generated and shared by you, is of course visible to whoever you share it with.

Your choices

You can update your profile at any time. If you want your data removed, contact us and we'll process the request.

Questions about this notice

Reach out through the contact page and select "General question."
