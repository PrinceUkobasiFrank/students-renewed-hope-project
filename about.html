About

Organized beats scattered.

The Students Renewed Hope Project is digital infrastructure for organizing the student network — not another place to post announcements into the void.

What this actually is

Students across the country already organize themselves — mostly through WhatsApp groups run by whoever stepped up first. That works, until it doesn't: no one knows how many students are actually involved, which states are active, or who to reach when it matters.

This platform doesn't replace those communities. It gives them a backbone: one place to register, one directory of state communities, one way to see — honestly — who's involved and where.

How it works

You register once with your name, state, and institution. That connects you to your state's community link and gives you a supporter card you can share. Coordinators, in turn, get a clear picture of their state instead of a guess based on group member counts.

What we don't do

We don't collect your National Identification Number. We don't touch voter registration — if you're looking to register to vote, we'll point you to the official process, not pretend to be it. We don't build a social network on top of WhatsApp; WhatsApp already exists and works.

How coordination works

  • Each state has its own coordinator and community link
  • National updates and policy go through the national team
  • States without a coordinator yet can nominate one

Get involved

Register, find your state, and generate your card — or reach out if you want to help coordinate.
